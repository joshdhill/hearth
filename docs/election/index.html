    Where the numbers come from

    Results and race calls come from civicAPI, which reads secretary-of-state feeds every few seconds and makes calls with its own decision desk. Its calls can come earlier or later than AP or network calls. Odds are live market prices, shown as each party's chance. Polymarket is read directly every 15 seconds. Kalshi doesn't let other websites read its prices, so a GitHub Action in the hearth repo copies them about once a minute and Kalshi can trail by a few minutes; the age next to its name says how fresh it is. Final polls are each pollster's latest survey from the last three weeks, averaged, from poll lists compiled on Wikipedia and VoteHub (snapshot Nov 3, 10 a.m. ET). Poll closing times come from 270toWin.
