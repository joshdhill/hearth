A beautiful traditional fireplace seen straight on, centred, in a cozy room in the evening. The fireplace surround is neat, well-kept red brick: even, clean bricks in a tidy running bond with fine light-grey mortar lines, a subtle warm colour, no damage or soot stains. A straight brick header of upright soldier-course bricks runs across the top of the firebox opening, brick jambs on either side, and a raised brick hearth in front with a clean edge. The brick around the opening is lit warmly by the fire; it falls into soft shadow further out. Photorealistic, sharp, symmetrical, calm and inviting.
